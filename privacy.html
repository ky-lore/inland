Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Inland Renovation & Construction ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you request an estimate, fill out a form, call, text or email us — such as your name, phone number, email address, project address and details about the work you're considering. We may also automatically collect basic technical information (such as your browser type, device and general location) through standard web analytics and cookies to help us understand how our site is used.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiries, prepare estimates, schedule consultations, complete and support your project, and communicate with you about your job. We may also use it to improve our website and services. We do not sell your personal information.

How We Share Information

We may share information with trusted third parties who help us operate our business — for example, scheduling, communication or analytics providers, and (only with your consent) financing partners such as Hearth. These parties are permitted to use your information only to provide services to us. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights, safety or property.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and measure site performance. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not function as intended.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by contacting us. You may also request that we update or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to any legal recordkeeping obligations.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact us at (951) 330-6784 or nicholasv@inlandconstruction.net, or write to us at 29265 3rd St, Lake Elsinore, CA 92532.
