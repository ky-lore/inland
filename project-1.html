01 The Project

One home, reimagined room by room.

This full-home remodel brought a single, elevated design language across the main living spaces. Custom rift white-oak Shaker cabinetry, brushed-brass hardware and fixtures, honed light-stone countertops and wide-plank wood-look flooring tie the kitchen, living room, wet bar and primary suite together.

The result is warm and timeless rather than trendy — a home that feels calm and cohesive from the moment you walk in, with the same level of craftsmanship carried into every room and every detail.
